Fix ARIMA forecast failure on irregular indexes

CI caught a Linux-only failure: when a dataset has missing values, the NaN
drop leaves gaps in the DatetimeIndex, and statsmodels then raises
"No supported index is available" from model.predict().

Fit ARIMA on a positional RangeIndex and keep the DatetimeIndex purely for
labelling the output. This preserves the existing drop-NaN-pairs semantics
(training_points still excludes the missing rows) and removes the
platform-dependent crash.
